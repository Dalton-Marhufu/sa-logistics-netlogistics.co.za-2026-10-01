## Top 3 Issues
- Generic default WordPress theme with out-of-the-box Gutenberg colours (light blue, green, purple) that ignore the brand's real charcoal and orange-red identity from its own logo.
- Duplicated hero content on the homepage (the same heading and paragraph rendered twice) and huge empty gaps, showing a page assembled by stacking blocks without a designed hierarchy.
- Weak conversion flow: the "Get A Quote" CTA is buried in the menu/footer, there is no WhatsApp contact, and the homepage gives no reason to act above the fold.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Rebuilt hero using the company's real tagline ("Your Reliable Partner in Cross-Border Logistics") with their actual truck and warehouse photography plus phone and quote CTAs above the fold.
- Full services grid using the site's exact service names (Transport to Zimbabwe, Transport to Zambia, Transporting Hazardous Materials, Logistics for Shipping Across Southern Africa, Cross-Border Freight Consolidation) each with real site imagery.
- Trust-focused design: RTMS certification badge, HAZCHEM fleet highlights, Google review signals, team contact details and a working quote form wired to their real contact details.
